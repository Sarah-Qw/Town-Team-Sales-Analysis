# Town-Team-Sales-Analysis

### **Overview**
* This project aims to analyze the sales data of Town Team for the period between 2019 and 2021[cite: 1].
* Total sales achieved amounted to $957,132 through 1,036 registered orders[cite: 1].
* The Casual Wear category led as the top-selling category[cite: 1].
* The average quantity per order was exactly 1 item[cite: 1].

### **Questions Answered**
* What is the total sales amount for the entire period?[cite: 1]
* What are the quantities sold and total revenue for each product, and which one is the "Volume Champion"?[cite: 1]
* What is the revenue generated from each category, and which category contributes the most to sales?[cite: 1]
* How are sales distributed between physical stores (On Shop) and online (Online Order)?[cite: 1]
* What is the monthly sales pattern, and are there seasonal fluctuations?[cite: 1]
* What are the top 5 products in terms of quantity and revenue?[cite: 1]
* What is the average selling price per item and per category?[cite: 1]
* How do sales change throughout the different days of the month?[cite: 1]
* What is the average number of items per order, and are there specific customer behavior patterns?[cite: 1]
* What is the most popular brand based on both sales volume and revenue?[cite: 1]
* How does the performance of Casual Wear compare to Semi-Formal wear?[cite: 1]

### **Tools Used**
* **Data Analysis:** Used Pivot Tables to extract Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as total sales and total orders[cite: 1].
* **Data Transformation:** Data cleaning and pre-processing tools to remove duplicates, format dates, and create calculated columns[cite: 1].
* **Data Visualization:** Dashboard building software (e.g., Power BI / Excel) to design interactive visual reports.

### **What I Did**

**1. Data Cleaning & Pre-processing:**
* Checked and removed duplicates in Receipt numbers to ensure no sales transaction was repeated[cite: 1].
* Handled missing values in the Selling-Price column by calculating the average for each product and filling the blanks[cite: 1].
* Standardized text formats in the Order-type column and removed extra spaces in item names using the TRIM function[cite: 1].
* Created calculated columns using formulas, such as extracting the day number, applying an exclusive 15% discount for (Jeans - Denim), and calculating the net unit price and total order amount[cite: 1].

**2. Designed the Dashboard (2 Pages):**
* Designed the first dashboard page (**Sales Distribution Dashboard**) to provide a comprehensive high-level overview featuring KPI cards, annual sales trends, and market share by category.
* Designed the second page (**Executive Summary / Detailed Insights**) to offer deeper analytical insights, such as product performance comparison, daily sales trends, and sales by order type.

**3. Key Insights:**
* The Casual Wear category dominates first place, generating approximately 63% of the company's total revenue[cite: 1].
* In-store sales (On Shop) account for a massive 93%, while the digital/online presence suffers from clear weakness[cite: 1].
* March is considered the company's "Golden Season." Additionally, daily purchasing peaks on the 1st of each month due to the "Payday Effect," and gradually declines to its lowest point on the 31st[cite: 1].
* The Jeans category (especially Levis and Denim brands) is the main profit driver and the true backbone of the company[cite: 1].

**4. Recommendations:**
* Target online customers with exclusive offers to capitalize on the massive growth opportunity in this underperforming segment[cite: 1].
* Focus on marketing operations and ensure sufficient inventory before March to maximize profits during the annual demand peak[cite: 1].
* Launch promotional offers and end-of-month discounts to boost sales during the stagnation periods that follow the payday effect[cite: 1].
